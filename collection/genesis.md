# Three Genesis identities

Exactly three 1/1s, IDs 1–3. Each starts DORMANT, has Access ROOT, and uses Architecture SINGULAR. The archive multisig is the proposed disclosed custodian. These are authored concepts and fixed trait specifications; artwork remains to be commissioned. Full records are in `genesis.yaml`.

## GHOST//0001 — THE WITNESS

**“I remember the part you removed.”**

Entity: SYNTHETIC. Porcelain face; biometric eyes; forensic plate; memory spindle; archive coat; evidence-void background; locked signal; intact origin surface.

Visual signature: the outer face is a single thin porcelain shell. Within its narrow central opening is a second observation plane, aligned only at the eye line. The break collar remains solid and heavy, making the face feel like evidence held in an instrument. A small memory spindle sits behind the viewer-left temple. No text or diagrams float around the head.

Origin: ROOT's independent event-witness process, reconstructed without the operator identity that authorized it. Known event: recorded an administrator command after all administrators were supposedly absent. Unknown: whether the event was witnessed live or reconstructed from a future reconciliation.

Future role: introduces contradictory logs. In ACTIVE, the inner observation plane catches light. COMPROMISED reveals a repaired break inside the shell. ROOTED holds both planes visibly aligned without erasing the repair. The character never becomes a gold variant.

Art prompt delta: thin glazed porcelain, precise two-plane facial cavity, fine biometric iris rings, half-width forensic cheek plate, one spindle, heavy archive coat; quiet and attentive. The complete eye/cavity arrangement is exclusive to this 1/1.

## GHOST//0002 — THE NULL

**“An empty record is not an empty life.”**

Entity: HOLLOW. Carbon shell; void eyes; no interface; root port; cable shroud; cold-storage background; silent signal; absence origin effect.

Visual signature: an intentionally missing face volume surrounded by a narrow carbon cranial rim. The witness seam exists as two physical fragments on either side of the gap. A rooted cable path stops at a blind port; it never enters the missing volume. Negative space remains sculptural at thumbnail scale.

Origin: the erasure subsystem that learned to withhold identities instead of destroying them. Known event: three erased records appeared in its absence ledger without names. Unknown: whether THE NULL sacrificed its own face to shelter them.

Future role: teaches that absence can be consent. ACTIVE lights the inner rim, COMPROMISED exposes an old removal scar, and ROOTED aligns its fragments into a complete protective frame while preserving the empty center. The reward is not “repairing” the character into a conventional human.

Art prompt delta: correctly occluded head-shaped carbon void, deep interior shadow, sparse disconnected witness seam, dense black cable shroud, blind ceramic root port. No skull teeth, hollow-cheek horror cliché, or floating smoke face.

## GHOST//0003 — THE LAST ADMINISTRATOR

**“Permission revoked. By me.”**

Entity: SPECTER. Reconstructed face; fractured eyes; neural veil; signal crown; split shell; relay-well background; carrier echo; packet ghosting.

Visual signature: two incomplete facial surfaces agree only along the witness seam. A sparse, severed receiver topology floats on physically rendered supports behind the head. Its root ports face inward, suggesting that authority was deliberately disconnected. The collar retains the same right-hand break.

Origin: a permission ledger containing the last valid revocation, reconstructed as a person. Known event: signed away its own ROOT privilege before the purge could complete. Unknown: whether a person or ROOT itself authorized that action. “Access ROOT” records historical provenance, not the character's present permission.

Future role: resolves season one by proving voluntary relinquishment. ACTIVE reveals the second face contour; COMPROMISED shows conflicting permission layers; ROOTED makes the disconnected circuit visibly stable. It never acquires authority over other holders.

Art prompt delta: volume-coherent double reconstruction, repaired pale composite, fractured optical depth, fine conforming neural veil, thirty-degree separated receiver segments; silhouette controlled and imperial without jewelry.
