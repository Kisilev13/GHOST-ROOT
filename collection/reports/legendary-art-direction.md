## Direction for the two open legendaries (for the authoring pass)

Both 1503 and 2803 are HOLLOW severed_halo. They must not repeat 0303/0803's
human/synthetic solutions, and must differ from each other:

- **1503** — lean into the hollow void as the subject: a full severed-halo ring
  *suspended around emptiness*, the ceramic collar broken and floating, cabling
  passing through the void where a face would be. Negative space is the composition.
- **2803** — a different structural idea entirely: e.g. the hollow shell cracked
  open to reveal internal archive racks / a spinal signal-crown column rising from
  the neck cavity, lit from within. Must carry real hardware, not a bare bust.

Both require authored illustration assets (as 0303/0803 received). Until they exist,
the batch cannot be approved.
