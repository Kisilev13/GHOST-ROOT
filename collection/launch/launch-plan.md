## Launch conduct

Publish the exact 201 reserve allocations, complete fixed artwork, four states, price, fees policy, contract address, retained authorities, license, and unsold-close behavior before mint. Keep a plain-language copy outside the fictional terminal. No fake progress counters, secret team rarity selection, manufactured partnership logos, or undisclosed promotion.

Prelaunch survey/critique participants should include nontechnical users and people who will not mint. Treat their interest as feedback, not a forecast of demand. Partner placements require actual agreement and disclosure; slots are uncommitted until a legitimate participant is selected.

Choose the final price only after a reviewed budget for art, engineering, independent security review, infrastructure, operations, legal work, and applicable taxes. Planning price is 0.004 ETH, not a live offer. Prefund essential delivery and 12-month archival operations; do not require a sellout or royalties to finish the promised season. If the budget is insufficient, narrow scope before sale and explain the changed terms.

## Unsold and incident policy

Sale closes at the fixed time whether sold out or not. Unminted IDs remain unminted and are not sold later through an admin path. Archive them as unrecovered portraits without calling them minted NFTs. Report actual issuance separately from the maximum edition. Never burn existing tokens to manufacture a different supply statistic.

If a material incident occurs, pause available mint paths, publish a plain-language incident notice with verified timestamps, and stop promotional scheduling. Do not roleplay a real compromise as an ARG. Resume only after the documented review criteria pass and only within the contract's existing sale window. No emergency DM addresses or surprise replacement collection.

## Operations after launch

Monitor mint/state error rates, metadata/file availability, pin health, marketplace indexing, RPC discrepancies, authority events, and collection impersonation. Use aggregate holder concentration only with a published purpose and minimal retention; never collect unrelated wallet history or real-user identity dossiers. Track account compromise/phishing through reports and owned-channel controls, without unauthorized probing of third parties.

Maintain release manifest, deployed addresses, authority matrix, source hashes, security review, incident runbook, and signed postmortems. Editorial expansion is optional and separately funded; no fungible token, yield, automatic merch entitlement, or perpetual content production is promised by season one.
