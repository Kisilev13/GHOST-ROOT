# GHOST//ROOT — collection thesis

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Name | GHOST//ROOT |
| Symbol | GHRT; provisional pending collision checks |
| Tagline | You were never supposed to find them. |
| Thesis | 3,333 recovered identities from a vanished security network become portraits of people choosing what the internet is allowed to remember. |
| Collector | People drawn to digital identity, speculative fiction, archival design, experimental fashion, and hands-on internet culture; technical ability is optional. |
| Visual hook | A broken ceramic collar and one horizontal forensic seam, repeated across four distinct kinds of face. |
| Cultural hook | Persistent identity in an internet increasingly populated by synthetic people, deleted histories, and unverifiable evidence. |
| Narrative hook | ROOT did not lose these identities. It may have hidden them from its own administrators. |
| Identity appeal | A composed, unreadable portrait plus a personal record of participation; the avatar works even without knowing the lore. |
| Defensibility | Consistent authored anatomy, a constrained material library, three contradictory narrators, and a finite published story create cumulative craft and continuity. None prevents imitation or guarantees demand. |
| Difference | Clean common portraits; geometry-based rarity; immutable origin traits; voluntary evolution; an archive experience that stays useful without a wallet. |

Retain the supplied name. No alternative-name exercise is needed while GHOST//ROOT is the chosen concept. Use `GHRT` rather than the local skill's suggested `GROOT` to avoid an unnecessary association with an established fictional character; this is a naming judgment, not clearance.

Before public launch, commission trademark/name clearance in intended markets, check domains, X handles, Discord server names, marketplace collection names, and confusing Unicode lookalikes. Handle candidates: `ghostroot`, `ghostrootarchive`, `ghostrootnet`. Availability and rights are unverified. Slashes belong in the wordmark, not a domain or handle. Do not announce a canonical domain until it is controlled.
